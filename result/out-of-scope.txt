# Out-of-scope assets observed (not tested)

This file records assets discovered during in-scope testing that were NOT tested, per
AGENT.md §2/§6. Scope is the ui.com domain and its explicitly approved components
(ui.com, www.ui.com, account.ui.com, sso.ui.com, account.svc.ui.com,
cloudaccess.svc.ui.com). Everything below is either a different registrable domain or a
third-party service.

## Third-party origins loaded by www.ui.com / account.ui.com
- cdn.cookielaw.org, geolocation.onetrust.com (OneTrust consent)
- www.googletagmanager.com, www.google-analytics.com (GTM/GA)
- static.hotjar.com (Hotjar)
- dev.visualwebsiteoptimizer.com (VWO)
- www.redditstatic.com, pixel-config.reddit.com, alb.reddit.com (Reddit pixel)
- js.stripe.com, m.stripe.network (Stripe)
- static.ada.support (Ada chat)
- images.svc.ui.com (image CDN; only static images requested)

## Other ui.com infrastructure (not in the approved component list)
- `*.svc.ui.com` services other than account.svc/cloudaccess.svc, e.g.
  fabric.svc, billing.svc, enterprise.svc, lte-api.svc, u5g-api.svc, megaphone.svc,
  geo.svc, images.svc, direct.svc, feedback.svc, account-cdn.svc
- `*.store.ui.com` / `store.ui.com` (Shopify storefront; separate commerce system)
- `*.ubnt.com`, `uisp.com`, `amplifi.com`, `unifi.com` and other non-ui.com domains
- 1,569 `ui.com` subdomains from certificate-transparency enumeration, dominated by
  `<uuid>.unifi-hosting[.stg|.dev].ui.com`. Passive enumeration only; no DNS brute force
  or host probing was performed (would be broad/out-of-scope and risk disruptive testing).

## Notes
- The existing `sso.ui.com` credentialed-CORS observation would only become exploitable
  if an attacker could control content on some `*.ui.com` origin. No such origin was
  found; wildcard `*.ui.com` serves a fixed SPA. See
  `result/findings/cors-credentialed-reflection.md`.
