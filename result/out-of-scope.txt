Authorization: `data/targets.txt` = `account.ui.com` (domain-only).

are related". The account.ui.com SPA is a static CloudFront/S3 app whose APIs are
all on other hosts. Active testing of the hosts below was therefore DEFERRED and
NOT performed; they were only observed as normal application traffic or read
from the client JavaScript configuration.
